How it works / tips
  • Everything runs locally in your browser — no video is uploaded anywhere.
  • Hold a playing card flat, filling a good chunk of the frame, against a plain background.
  • Good, even lighting with no glare helps detection a lot.
  • When a card-shaped rectangle is detected steadily for a moment, a different card is overlaid on top of it for about 3 seconds, then the view returns to your normal camera feed.
  • If auto-detection isn't cooperating (e.g. bad lighting), use the "Trigger Effect (demo)" button to see the reveal animation on demand.
